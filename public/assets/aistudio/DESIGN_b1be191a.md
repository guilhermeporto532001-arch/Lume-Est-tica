## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, clinical luxury aesthetic engineered specifically for high-end aesthetic medicine, dermatology clinics, and holistic skincare practices. The visual language bridges clinical precision and soothing sensory wellness, stripping away the sterile hostility of legacy EHR/practice management software in favor of an organic, welcoming atmosphere.

The core design movement combines **organic soft UI and tactile neumorphism** with **sculptural pill containers**. Surfaces feel carved, cushioned, and tactile, using creamy undertones, hyper-diffused ambient light, and delicate chromatic pastels. Every interaction delivers emotional reassurance, tranquility, and refined elegance, fostering confidence for clinicians and a calming sanctuary for patients.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in soft botanical minerals and warm skin-derived undertones, intentionally steering clear of stark cold greys.

- **Primary (`#7A9A8B`)**: Muted sage herb. Evokes clinical calm, natural recovery, and scientific organic care. Serves as the primary touchpoint color for active states, key callouts, and brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#B99A65`)**: Warm champagne gold. Conveys premium craftsmanship, subtle prestige, VIP badges, and luxury client loyalty tiers.
- **Tertiary (`#E08F7E`)**: Soft terracota blush. Infuses warmth and human vitality, utilized for alerts, notifications, schedule urgencies, and warm highlights.
- **Neutral (`#24332C`)**: Deep botanical slate. High-contrast yet gentler than pitch black, ensuring crisp legibility while harmonizing with the sage and linen ecosystem.

### Foundation & Pastel Functional Tones
- **Canvas Base**: Warm Off-White (`#FBF9F5`) and Luminous Porcelain (`#FFFEFC`).
- **Pastel Container Tints**:
  - *Sálvia Suave* (`#EBF2EC`): Clinical protocols, healthy records, completed appointments.
  - *Pêssego Rose* (`#FDEEE9`): Facial aesthetics, personalized skin therapy, pending follow-ups.
  - *Champagne Sand* (`#F4ECE1`): Body remodeling, packages, billing summaries.
  - *Sutil Iris/Lavender* (`#EFF2F9`): Consultations, diagnostics, holistic wellness logs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Epilogue** for display, headlines, and prominent card titles with **Manrope** for body copy, tabular records, clinical forms, and UI microcopy.

- **Epilogue** contributes an editorial, sculptured character with warm proportions and distinctive letterforms that give patient records and clinic dashboards an upscale boutique identity.
- **Manrope** introduces geometric balance and high legible clarity across treatment dosages, calendar timetables, patient histories, and interactive buttons.
- Display sizes gracefully transition across mobile viewports to prevent unwanted line wrapping on compact handheld device screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an airy, breathable grid designed to eliminate clutter and reduce cognitive fatigue for clinic practitioners:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column responsive grid with a fluid maximum canvas width of 1440px, anchored by `margin-desktop` (3rem) and generous column `gutter-desktop` (2rem).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters, allowing side-by-side card arrangements for calendar schedules and treatment notes.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: Single column with `margin` (1.25rem) margins and 1rem gutters, optimizing vertical scrolling for daily routine views and rapid patient check-ins.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card paddings rely on `space-md` (1.25rem) and `space-lg` (2rem) to preserve an open, cushion-like perimeter around patient data and timeline markers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **organic soft-UI depth structure** rather than flat borders or dramatic dark drop shadows:

- **Ambient Luminescence (Base Elevation)**: Cards rest above the `#FBF9F5` base using dual micro-shadows: a diffuse bottom drop shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(122, 154, 139, 0.08)`) and an internal highlight feather (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), creating a cushion-soft, touchable extrusion.
- **Floating Navigation Dock**: Floating bottom tab bars and modal panels utilize light refraction with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), frosted white transparency (`rgba(255, 254, 252, 0.85)`), and elevated ambient diffusion (`0 16px 36px -6px rgba(36, 51, 44, 0.07)`).
- **Tonal Insets**: Progress tracks, habit check grids, and unselected date cells are visually debossed into the canvas using soft inner shadows (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-rounded, organic curvature that creates a warm, cocooning ambiance:

- **Base Corner Radii (`roundedness: 3`)**: Interactive pills, buttons, and status tags are completely rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px). Standard clinical cards and data modules feature a pronounced radius (`2rem` to `2.5rem`), echoing the soft, sculpted surfaces of modern wellness architectural spaces.
- **Nested Ratio Discipline**: When small tags or circular date markers are placed within larger parent containers, the child elements maintain harmonious concentric curvature, eliminating sharp visual tangents throughout the interface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with `#7A9A8B`, crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle top-edge bevel highlight, accompanied by a soft colored shadow (`0 6px 16px rgba(122, 154, 139, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary / Soft**: Pill-shaped container rendered in champagne pastel (`#F4ECE1`) or sage wash (`#EBF2EC`) with `#24332C` body typography.
- **Floating Icon Buttons**: Perfect circles (48px / 56px) hosting minimal 1.5px stroke icons, finished with a subtle off-white gloss.

### Cards & Habit/Procedure Modules
- **Procedure & Treatment Cards**: Structured with `2rem` radii, surfaced in thematic pastels (*Sálvia*, *Pêssego*, *Champagne*, or *Iris*). Cards contain an icon badge, treatment title, duration counter, and a circular trailing completion indicator.
- **Interactive States**: Cards gently depress on tap (`transform: scale(0.99)`) with softened shadow diffusion.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Chips**: Ultra-rounded micro-containers (`rounded-full`, 8px vertical padding, 16px horizontal padding).
- **Categorization**: Utilizes low-saturation tint backgrounds paired with high-contrast neutral-dark text for effortless scanning of treatment statuses (e.g., "Confirmado", "Em Andamento", "Avaliação").

### Calendar & Habit Matrix
- **Date Selector**: Horizontal pill ribbons or circular date nodes. Current day is framed in an ultra-fine accent ring (`#B99A65` or `#E08F7E`), while completed treatment days reveal soft colored fills with delicate laurel or check icons.
- **Grid Tracking**: Aesthetic evaluation trackers (e.g., skin hydration, peeling stages) use soft rounded-rect micro-cells that change from pale off-white to saturated pastel sage as progress advances.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs**: Cushion-pill inputs filled with `#FFFEFC`, inset contour borders (`1px solid rgba(122, 154, 139, 0.15)`), and smooth sage focus halos (`0 0 0 4px rgba(122, 154, 139, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Smooth rounded squares (`0.75rem`) and circular radios with central pill checks in sage and terracotta tones.

### Floating Bottom Navigation Bar
- A detached, pill-shaped dock centered at the bottom of the viewport (`height: 64px`, `rounded-full`). Surfaced in milky glass with high-level blur, holding clean geometric icons with warm active indicator pills.
